Roz kitna practice karein

Typing kaise seekhe - step by step

Typing seekhna mushkil nahi hai, bas sahi order me practice karni hoti hai. Chiku Type ke lessons aapko ek-ek step karke aage le jaate hain.

Step 1: Sahi tareeka aur home row

  • Peeth seedhi rakhein, kalai dheeli aur screen aankhon ke saamne.
  • Ungliyan home row par rakhein: A S D F aur J K L ;
  • Pehle sirf home row ke lessons karein, keyboard dekhe bina.

Step 2: Upar aur neeche ki rows

  • Har ungli ko uski tay key dein aur baaki rows ke lessons ek-ek karke karein.
  • Galti hone par ruk kar sahi key dobara dabayein, jaldi na karein.

Step 3: Shift, numbers aur punctuation

Capital letters, numbers aur symbols ke lessons se poora keyboard cover ho jata hai.

Step 4: Hindi typing

  • Pehle ek layout chunein: Kruti Dev 010, Remington Gail ya Mangal (InScript).
  • Pehle matra aur aadhe akshar ke lessons karein, kyunki Hindi me galti inhi par hoti hai.

Step 5: Speed aur accuracy

Pehle 95 percent se upar accuracy laayein, uske baad speed badhayein. Net speed me galtiyan ghat jaati hain.

Step 6: Exam practice

Lessons ke baad 10 minute ka timed test dein, jaise SSC jaise exam me hota hai.

Roz ka plan

10 minute lesson, 15 minute typing test aur 5 minute galat shabdon ka dobara practice.

Leaderboard guide: Bihar, Katihar top typist aur typing record

Chiku Type ke leaderboard par aap dekh sakte hain ki typing speed me kaun aage hai. Bihar ka typing record ho ya Katihar ka No. 1 typer, apna score save karke aap bhi is list me aa sakte hain.

Leaderboard par apna naam kaise laayein

  • Profile banayein aur apni location (state aur city ya district) set karein.
  • Typing test dein. Aapka best WPM aur accuracy leaderboard me judta hai.
  • Roz practice karke apna rank badhayein aur apne local leaderboard par upar aayein.

Local ranking kyun khaas hai

Aapki location ke hisaab se local leaderboard dikhta hai, isliye apne shehar ya zile me No. 1 banna sabse aasan hai. Bihar aur Katihar ke typist apne yahan ki list me mukabla kar sakte hain.

Record ke baare me

Ye ranking Chiku Type par diye gaye tests ke best scores par based hai, koi official record nahi.

Frequently asked questions

Is Chiku Type free?

Yes. You can take typing tests, practise Hindi and English typing and follow lessons without paying.

Which Hindi typing layouts are available?

Kruti Dev 010, Remington Gail and Mangal (InScript). You type on a normal English keyboard and each key is converted to the Hindi layout you pick.

Can I prepare for the SSC typing test here?

Yes. Choose a 10 minute test in English or Hindi and practise against the usual CHSL LDC/JSA targets of 35 WPM English or 30 WPM Hindi. Always confirm the latest rules in the official SSC notification.

What is a good typing speed?

For most government exams 30 to 35 WPM is the target. Above 45 WPM is considered good for everyday work.
