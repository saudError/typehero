How the typing test works

  3. When the timer ends you get your speed in WPM, your accuracy and a speed-over-time chart.
  4. Press Tab for a new test, or repeat the same words to beat your own score.

What is WPM and how is it calculated?

WPM means words per minute. In typing tests one word is counted as five characters, including spaces, so a fast typist is not punished for typing long words.

Gross WPM is the total characters typed divided by five, divided by the minutes. Net WPM subtracts mistakes, which is why accuracy matters as much as raw speed. Many exams only count your net speed.

These are rough ranges. Government exam targets are usually in the 30 to 35 WPM range, so most people only need steady practice, not extreme speed.

Tips to improve your typing speed

  • Keep your fingers on the home row (A S D F and J K L ;) and use all ten fingers.
  • Accuracy first. Speed comes automatically once mistakes stop slowing you down.
  • Practise 15 to 20 minutes every day instead of one long weekly session.
  • Use the Lessons section for step-by-step finger training before you chase high scores.
  • Sit straight, keep your wrists relaxed and look at the screen, not the keys.

Practice paragraph

Regular practice is the quickest way to improve. Sit comfortably, keep your hands relaxed, and type each word carefully before you try to go faster. A steady rhythm with few mistakes will always beat a fast start that ends in errors.
